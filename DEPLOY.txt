SCHOLARHUB - how to put it online (all files go in the SAME folder on your host)

index.html            Student and teacher app (the installable app)
admin.html            Admin portal (separate, not linked from the student app)
arcade.html           Quizzes, games, exam papers, ScholarHub Bot (opens inside the app)
papers.json           The 8 exam papers with answers (read as documents)
sw.js                 Makes the app work offline
manifest.webmanifest  Makes the app installable and full screen
icon-*.png            App icons
firestore.rules       Paste into Firebase > Firestore > Rules > Publish

Must be served over https (Firebase Hosting, Netlify, GitHub Pages...). Installing and
offline mode need https. After the first visit online, the app opens without internet.
